⏱ About 5 minutes🔁 Once a yearYou need: an empty service log
Stop 2 of 6
Why: the app builds its school calendar from an empty copy of the service log: which days are school days, and which row each date sits on, so it can tell you exactly which cell to paste into. It only reads the dates; it never changes the real log.
1

Download an empty service log

Open a blank service log workbook in Google Sheets: the district's empty template, or a fresh copy with no student filled in. Choose File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx). The dates are the same in every log, so an empty one works and keeps student information off your computer.

2

Settings → Calendar ingest → Choose a service log file…

Pick the .xlsx you just downloaded (it's in your Downloads folder).

3

Check the preview, then Confirm and save calendar

It names the tab it read (ELA, Math, SS, or SC), how many dates (about 167), and the four nine-weeks with their first cells. A yellow note about a merged cell just means: watch that cell when you paste. Then click Confirm and save calendar.

✓ You'll know it worked

  • The line Saved: 167 dates from the "ELA" tab.
  • The Entry tab shows the School year.
  • On a school day, the not a school day tag next to the date is gone.

✕ Avoid these

  • A workbook with no ELA, Math, SS, or SC tab. The app reads the first of those it finds.
  • Typing over dates in the file. Use the workbook as it is.
