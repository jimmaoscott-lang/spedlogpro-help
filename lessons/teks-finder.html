Set up a TEKS Finder

⏱ About 5 minutes🔁 Once per courseYou need: a Claude account and your course's TEKS
Why: a goal raises alerts only when its TEKS codes match the lessons you log. A TEKS Finder is a Claude project that already holds your course's TEKS, so you paste just the goal's wording and get the matching codes back, in the short form SpEd Log Pro uses.
1

Get the prompt

This short set of instructions turns a Claude project into a TEKS Finder. Your course's TEKS go in once, so after that you only paste a goal.

🎯 TEKS Finder prompt
1 page · any subject · you fill in your course and paste your TEKS once
Download as text
2

In Claude, make a project for your course

Go to claude.ai, click Projects in the left sidebar, then Create project (it may say New project). Name it for the course, like TEKS Finder (English 3). Teach more than one subject? Make one per course, since each has its own TEKS.

3

Paste the prompt into the project's instructions

Inside the project, open Instructions (it may say Set project instructions), paste the whole prompt, and save.

4

Fill in your course and paste your TEKS, once

Change Course: [type your course here, …] to your course. Then replace the last line, [Paste the complete, current TEKS for your course here …], with your course's TEKS from the Texas Education Agency's TEKS pages (the course introduction and every knowledge-and-skills statement). Save. You won't paste the TEKS again unless they change.

5

Use it: paste a goal, copy the codes

Start a chat inside the project and paste the goal's wording, with the name replaced by "the student". The first line of the answer is the codes, ready to paste, like 28A; 28B, followed by a short table saying why each fits. Check them, then paste the first line into TEKS code(s) when you set up goal tracking.

⚠ Never paste a student's name, ID, birthday, or anything else that identifies them. Copy only the goal's wording, and replace the name with "the student" first. If a name slips in, the TEKS Finder won't repeat it and will remind you.

✓ You'll know it worked

  • The answer's first line is only codes, like 28A; 28B.
  • Pasted into TEKS code(s), they raise alerts when a lesson you log uses one of them.

✕ Avoid these

  • Pasting names or student details. The goal's wording is all it needs.
  • Taking every code on trust. Check that each fits the goal before you save it.
  • Chatting outside the project. A chat outside it doesn't have your TEKS.
