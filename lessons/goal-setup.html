Goals

Set up goal tracking

⏱ About 6 minutes🔁 Once per goal, revised after an ARDYou need: the IEP goal and its mastery criteria
Why: the app tracks one academic goal per student. You decide how you'll measure it; the app reminds you when a lesson touches it and keeps the evidence and running percentages. It never decides mastery for you.
1

Click the IEP Goal line, then Set up goal tracking

On the student's card, click IEP Goal. The Goal tracking pop-up opens; click Set up goal tracking.

2

Fill in the goal

The goal text comes from your roster. Add a Mastery note in plain words (required). TEKS code(s): the codes whose lessons count as evidence, separated by ; or ,; leave it blank if you'll measure by observation. Target: a fixed date, or Annual ARD (it shows the Next ARD date). Effective is when tracking starts. Percentage counts as: how the percentages are worked out (see below).

How to write a TEKS code: use the short form, the knowledge-and-skills number plus the letter, with nothing else.
Full codeType
113.41(d)(28)(B)28B
111.39(c)(5)(A)5A
two codes28A; 28B
It's the same form the TEKS box on a period page uses and the lesson picker fills in, and a code has to match exactly to raise an alert: 28 does not match 28B. Capitals and spaces don't matter.
Not sure which TEKS fit a goal? Ask an AI chat such as Claude. Paste the goal text and your course's TEKS, and ask: "Which student expectations in these TEKS does this IEP goal practice? Give the short codes, like 28B." Check what it suggests before you type it in. Tired of pasting the TEKS every time? Make a project that keeps them and fills this pop-up in for you: Set up a Goal Setup projectNEWfor version 0.4.20 and beyond, then import its file.
⚠ Never paste a student's name, ID, birthday, or anything else that identifies them. Copy only the goal's wording, and replace the name with "the student" first.
Percentage counts as: pick one. It decides how the card's Last 5 and All-time percentages are worked out from your checklist and trial-fraction entries. Example: one day 9 of 10 (90%), another day 2 of 2 (100%).
ChoiceHow it countsThe example
Total checked over total possibleAdds up everything checked or correct, over everything possible. A bigger entry counts more.11 of 12 = 92%
Plain average of each entry's percentAverages each entry's own percent. Every entry counts the same, however big.(90% + 100%) ÷ 2 = 95%
Total over possible suits a goal measured in items or trials ("80% of problems"). Plain average suits a goal measured per session ("80% in 4 of 5 opportunities"). Rubric picks and notes never count toward either. You can change it on a revision; the newest setting works out every entry again.
3

Pick how you'll measure it

Click the Tracking methods you'll use: a Checklist of criteria, Rubric levels, and/or a Trial fraction (correct of total). Add checklist items with + Add item, or click a starter idea. A typed note is always available too. Then Save goal.

4

Tracking has started

The pop-up shows what's being tracked. On the card, the IEP Goal title turns green.

✓ You'll know it worked

  • Tracking since … with the goal, mastery note, TEKS, and target.
  • A green IEP Goal on the card.

✕ Avoid these

  • Codes run together. 28a.29b reads as one code: separate them with ; or , (a yellow warning appears).
  • Expecting a mastery verdict. The app shows the evidence; you decide.
