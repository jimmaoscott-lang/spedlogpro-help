Set your bell schedule

⏱ About 3 minutes🔁 Once a yearYou need: your period times
Stop 3 of 6
Why: each period's length is the most minutes the app will credit a student for one day in that period, and it's the room time behind each card's surplus number. The times on a new install are placeholders (50 minutes each), so set yours.
1

Settings → Bell schedule

Click Settings, then the Bell schedule title to open it.

2

Type each period’s start and end time

Click the hour, type it, then the minutes, then AM or PM. The Length column updates as you type, so you can check each period comes out right.

3

Click Save bell schedule

The line under it says Saved.

✓ You'll know it worked

  • Saved. under the button.
  • Each period's Length matches your real bell schedule.

✕ Avoid these

  • Leaving the placeholders. Fifty-minute periods that aren't real give wrong surplus numbers.
  • Mixing up AM and PM. An end time before the start time is refused.

⚠ If you see this

A red message after SaveA period is missing a time, or ends before it starts. Fix that row and save again.
