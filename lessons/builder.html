Set up the Leveled Guide Builder

⏱ About 10 minutes🔁 OnceYou need: a Claude account and your course's TEKS
Why: the Leveled Guide Builder is a Claude project that turns one lesson into three leveled guides, a large-print set, a vocabulary sheet, a teacher guide, and a TEKS report, plus the two files SpEd Log Pro imports so it can fill in the TEKS and suggest each student's accommodations.
1

Get a prompt: quick, or made for your class

The prompt is the set of instructions that turns Claude into the Leveled Guide Builder. Pick one:

⚡ Quick: the ready-made prompt
9 pages · any subject (math, English, science, social studies) · you fill in your course, grade, and large print yes/no, and paste your TEKS
Open the PDF Download as text
🛠 Custom: the Prompt Maker
About 10 minutes · Claude asks you questions with choices, then writes a prompt fitted to your subject, curriculum, and lessons
Download as text
  1. In Claude, start a new chat (an ordinary one, not inside a project).
  2. Paste the Prompt Maker, attach one typical lesson or assignment (no student names), and send.
  3. Answer its questions. Each one gives choices and marks the one it recommends; pick one or type your own.
  4. When it writes your prompt, copy the whole block. That's the prompt you paste in step 3, with your course, grade, and large-print choice already filled in. You still paste your TEKS (step 5).

Copy buttons copy the whole thing, clean. Copying out of the PDF can break the lines oddly; the PDF is for reading or printing.

2

In Claude, make a new project

Go to claude.ai and sign in. Click Projects in the left sidebar, then Create project (it may say New project). Name it Leveled Guide Builder and create it.

3

Paste the prompt into the project's instructions

Inside the project, find Instructions (it may say Set project instructions) and click it. Paste the whole prompt and save. Project instructions apply to every chat you start in this project, so you do this once.

4

Fill in your course, grade, and large print

At the top of what you pasted, under Course setup, replace the three brackets. (A Prompt Maker prompt has these filled in already: skip to step 5.)

Course: [type your course here, …] → Course: Biology
Grade level: [type the grade here, …] → Grade level: 9th grade
Large print: [type yes or no] → Large print: no

Large print is optional. Yes makes a large-print Level 2 guide and the reading pages enlarged. No skips both, and the Guide Supports file leaves out Level 2 Large Print; SpEd Log Pro simply never uses its L2 LP button.

5

Paste your course's TEKS at the very bottom

The last line says [Paste the complete, current TEKS for your course here …]. Replace that line with your course's TEKS: the course introduction and every knowledge-and-skills statement with its student expectations. You'll find them on the Texas Education Agency's TEKS pages (Texas Administrative Code, Chapters 110 to 130). The builder cites codes only from what you paste here, so paste the whole course, and update it when the TEKS change. Save the instructions.

6

Use it: upload a lesson

Start a new chat inside the project and add the lesson document (drag it in, or use the paperclip or +). The builder starts right away and makes ten files (eight without large print): the three leveled guides, large print if you chose it, vocabulary, a teacher guide, a TEKS report, and two CSVs. Download them all.

Keep … - TEKS Alignment.csv and … - Guide Supports.csv in the same folder, then follow Leveled guides: set upNEWfor version 0.4.18 and beyond to bring them into SpEd Log Pro.

✓ You'll know it worked

  • The first reply lists the lesson title, the reading pages, the handout pages, and the key words, then the files follow.
  • Ten files (eight without large print), named with the lesson title, including … - TEKS Alignment.csv and … - Guide Supports.csv.

✕ Avoid these

  • Changing the support names in Step 8. SpEd Log Pro refuses a Guide Supports file with any other name.
  • Uploading anything with student names. The builder only needs the lesson.
  • Chatting outside the project. A chat outside it doesn't have the instructions.
