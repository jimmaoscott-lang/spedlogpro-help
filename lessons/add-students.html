Add your students

⏱ About a minute per student🔁 Once per studentYou need: your roster in the Roster folder, or your roster Sheet open
Stop 5 of 6
Why: the app only shows students you add, one at a time, on the period tab they belong to. It takes their minutes, accommodations, and goal from your roster, so there's nothing to retype. There are two ways in: pick from the roster CSV (Part 1), or paste the student's row straight from your Google Sheet (Part 2), handy for a new arrival when you haven't downloaded the CSV again.
Part 1

Pick from your roster

1

Click the student’s period tab

For example P2. There's no period to choose anywhere else: it's the tab you're on.

2

Type part of their name, and pick them

Click in District ID or name and type a few letters of the name or ID. Your roster drops down. Click the student.

3

Check the start date, then Confirm add

The line shows what the app found and which period it's adding to. Effective start is the first day on your caseload: leave it for a student who was there from the start of school, or change it for a mid-year arrival.

4

Repeat for each student

Each one on their own period tab. A student who's in two of your classes needs a little extra: see A student in two classes.

Part 2

Paste from your Google Sheet

Why: a new student shows up mid-year. Add their row to your roster Sheet, then paste that one row into the app. No new CSV to download. It also works for a student already in the app: you get the same change review as an ARD change.
5

On the student's period tab, click Paste from Sheets…

It's beside Add Student. The student goes on the tab you're on.

6

Copy the row in your Sheet, then paste it in the box

In your Google Sheet, click the student's row number (the grey number at the left) to select the whole row, and press Ctrl+C. Back in the app, click in the box and press Ctrl+V. Each field shows in its own labeled box, so you can check it and fix it before anything is added. Accommodations show the chips they'll become.

7

Check the start date, then Add to Period …

Scroll down. A date from your Sheet shows where it came from (Read from 4/1/2027 in your Sheet.). Check Start date, the first day on your caseload, then click Add to Period 2 (the button names your tab). Your fixes change only what goes into the app; your Sheet and CSV are never changed.

8

The card appears

A green line names who was added and from when, and their card is on the tab. From here they're an ordinary student, however they got in.

9

Already in the app? You get the change review

Paste the row of a student you already have (say, after an ARD raised their minutes) and the box lists each difference, In the app now beside Pasted, with a tick box. Untick anything you don't want, set the Effective date, and click Save approved changes. A row that matches exactly says No changes. More in Enter an ARD change.

✓ You'll know it worked

  • The line Added Noodle, Penelope Ann (0093310) to period 2, effective …
  • The student's card appears on that tab.
  • Pasting: Added Crumpet, Biscuit Lee (0099010) to period 2, effective …

✕ Avoid these

  • The wrong tab. Open the card's gear: Move to another period, or Delete permanently if nothing is logged yet.
  • An old roster. New students only show once the new CSV is in the Roster folder. Or paste their row instead (Part 2).
  • Moving columns in your Sheet. Paste names each cell from your CSV's headings, in order. Changed the Sheet's columns? Download the CSV again first.
  • Pasting two rows. One student at a time.
