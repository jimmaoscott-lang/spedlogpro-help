The Minutes report

⏱ About 4 minutes🔁 Whenever you want to checkYou need: some logged weeks
Why: it shows, week by week, whether a student's minutes were met, with the reason for every adjustment written out in words, so you never have to work it out yourself.
1

Settings → Minutes report → Per student

Click in District ID and pick the student from the list (type part of a name). Pick From and To, then Compute.

2

Read each week

One card per school week, with a badge: Compliant, Short by …, or Provisional — week not finished while a day is still unlogged. The sentence underneath says why, and a table shows each day.

Each weekWhat it means
RequiredThe weekly IEP minutes, for the days the student was enrolled
AdjustedRequired, minus each AB, O, S, SUB, or ARD day. SAB and FLEX don't lower it.
CreditedThe minutes printed in column B: spread over the days served, never more than the period's length in a day
ShortCredited is less than Adjusted
3

A whole class at once: Per class (period)

Pick the period and the range, then Compute: one table, one row per student per week. A short week is shaded, and Notes says why (here, two flex days that don't lower the minutes owed).

✓ You'll know it worked

  • A card per week (per student), or a table with a line saying how many students, weeks, and rows it listed (per class).

✕ Avoid these

  • Reading too much into a provisional week. It changes as you log the rest of it.
  • SAB or FLEX for anything else. They leave the minutes owed, so the week reads short.

⚠ If you see this

District ID, a start date, and an end date are all required.Fill in the missing box and Compute again.No in-session dates this week fall inside the student's enrollment window — nothing is owed.A break week, or a week the student wasn't enrolled. Nothing to do.
